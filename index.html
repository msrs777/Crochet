WHAT WE TEACH

From Basic Crochet to Beautiful Creations

🧶

Crochet Basics

Learn how to hold the crochet hook and yarn correctly, make basic stitches, stCroche projects and finish your work neatly.

📖

Pattern Reading

Understand crochet patterns, basic crochet symbols, stitch counts and how to follow simple designs.

🌸

Crochet Flowers

Learn to create beautiful crochet flowers including basic flowers, roses and different decorative flower designs.

🎀

Bow & HeCroche

Learn how to make cute crochet bows and heCroches in different sizes and designs.

☕

Crochet Coaster

Learn how to create a beautiful coaster while understanding increasing stitches, shape formation and proper finishing.

🟫

Granny Square

Learn how to stCroche a granny square, work in rounds, change colours, maintain the shape and finish it beautifully.

Basic Crochet Basic Stitches Pattern Reading Flowers Rose Bow HeCroche Coaster Granny Square
